## Reach Toone from your iPhone

The mobile companion connects to the Toone instance running on your Mac. Use it when you need to follow or control work without staying directly in front of the desktop. The mobile companion is not available in the Mac App Store edition.

```toone-ui
mobile-companion-home
```

## Connect through the cloud

Start mobile pairing in **Settings → Mobile** and enter the displayed code on the mobile device. The phone always connects through the relay, so it works whether or not the phone and Mac are on the same network.

```toone-ui
settings-mobile-pairing
```

## Approve and revoke devices

A device must complete the pairing handshake before it is accepted. Settings lists approved devices and lets you revoke one device or all of them when access should be removed.

```toone-ui
settings-mobile-devices
```
