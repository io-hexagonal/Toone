## Talk directly to an agent

Voice input lets you describe work, refine an idea, or respond to an agent without translating every thought into typed instructions first. The conversation remains connected to the active project and thread.

## Capture a meeting

Record microphone and system audio when a live conversation contains decisions or context the project should retain. Speaker labels help distinguish who contributed each part of the discussion.

## Turn conversation into useful work

Captured speech becomes valuable when it produces a durable result. Ask an agent to organize decisions, unresolved questions, and next actions, then keep that output with the project.

## Review before relying on it

Meeting capture can contain transcription mistakes or ambiguous statements. Review important decisions and assigned actions before treating the generated record as authoritative.
