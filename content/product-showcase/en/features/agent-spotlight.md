## What it does

Agent Spotlight gives you a fast way to summon an agent without first navigating through the full Toone workspace. Open it over the work already on your screen, find the agent or thread you need, and continue from there.

## Start from the work in front of you

The useful context for a request is often outside Toone: a document, a browser page, a design, or another application. Spotlight keeps the transition into an agent conversation short so you do not have to rearrange the desktop before asking for help.

## Choose the right destination

Use the project and agent identity shown in Spotlight to confirm where the conversation belongs. This keeps the resulting thread attached to the correct body of work instead of becoming an isolated prompt.

## When to use it

Agent Spotlight is useful for quick questions, continuing an active thread, or handing the next piece of work to a specialized agent while another application remains in focus.
